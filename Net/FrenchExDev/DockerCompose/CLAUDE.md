## Notes for Claude
- Two distinct codegen pipelines coexist here: BinaryWrapper SG (CLI wrapper) and ComposeBundleGenerator (compose-spec models). They are unrelated.
- The wrapper targets the standalone `docker-compose` binary, NOT `docker compose` (CLI plugin subcommand).
- Bundle SG delegates builder emission to `Builder.SourceGenerator.Lib` — never roll a new builder emitter here.
- The Design project uses 2-phase pipelined scraping with **eager** image cleanup (per-version, not batch). Disk usage stays proportional to `--parallel`.
- Help parsing uses `.UseParser("cobra")` — no `DockerComposeHelpParser` exists or should.
- Compose schemas live in `Bundle/schemas/` as `<AdditionalFiles>` and are committed. Re-run `Bundle.Design` manually to bump.
- `ComposeFile` and other models always carry `Dictionary<string, object?>? Extensions` for `x-*` keys.
