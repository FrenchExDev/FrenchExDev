Block on one or more containers

Description:
  Block until one or more containers stop and then print their exit codes.


Usage:
  podman wait [options] CONTAINER [CONTAINER...]

Examples:
  podman wait --interval 5s ctrID
  podman wait ctrID1 ctrID2

Options:
      --condition string   Condition to wait on (default "stopped")
  -i, --interval string    Time Interval to wait before polling for completion (default "250ms")
